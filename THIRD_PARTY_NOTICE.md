# Third-Party Components

PulseGuard uses third-party open-source components and container images, including technologies such as Docker, PostgreSQL, Prometheus, Grafana, Locust, Toxiproxy, FastAPI, and their transitive dependencies.

Before publishing or distributing a release:

- review the licenses of the exact versions in the repository
- retain required notices
- avoid copying third-party logos or documentation without permission
- verify that container redistribution is allowed
- document material modifications where required

Wikimedia EventStreams is used as a public activity signal. PulseGuard is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Wikimedia Foundation.
