The loop

A complete incident lifecycle, not just a chatbot

PulseGuard demonstrates the control points required to move from detection to accountable action.

01 ObserveCollect service and traffic telemetry.
02 DetectConfirm abnormal latency, errors, or availability.
03 InvestigateCorrelate bounded evidence and topology.
04 DecideRecommend a specific response with rationale.
05 GovernApply allowlist and approval policy.
06 ActExecute the approved controlled action.
07 VerifyMeasure recovery from telemetry.
08 ResolveClose or assign to support with evidence.
